Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you an OEM manufacturer or a distributor?

We are a North American OEM manufacturer with 13 years of experience. Our equipment is engineered, machined, assembled, and tested in our own facilities in the United States. We are not a reseller.

Do you sell directly to end users?

No. Cryovanta operates strictly through a network of authorized distributors, OEMs, and industrial suppliers. We do not sell directly to end users. This policy ensures that our partners maintain protected margins and receive our full factory support.

Can your engineers help select the right equipment for my facility?

Yes. We do not use a standard catalog. Our application engineers will review your fluid type, flow rate, pressure, and site conditions to specify the exact pump or transmitter for your process.

Can you build custom skids and manifolds?

Absolutely. We specialize in custom turn-key skids, including Coriolis mass flow meters, PLC controls, custom ASME flanges, and specialized alloys (like Hastelloy or Tantalum) for aggressive fluids.

Are your pumps tested before shipment?

Yes. Every pump undergoes rigorous hydrostatic testing. For our heavy-duty cryogenic pumps, we utilize an actual LNG test facility to verify performance and thermal contraction at −162°C before shipment.

Do you offer spare parts, repairs, and field service?

Yes. We provide fast access to OEM spare parts, in-house repair services, and on-site commissioning support. Our engineers are available directly to troubleshoot any operational issues.

What is your typical lead time?

Standard equipment is typically 4–6 weeks. Custom-engineered skids and large terminal pumps vary by project scope, but our engineering team provides a firm delivery schedule with every quotation.

How do I request a quote or technical datasheet?

You can click the “Get a Quote” button at the top right of any page. When you submit your project details, a Cryovanta engineer will contact you directly within 24 hours.
