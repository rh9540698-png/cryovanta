Overview

Overview

The SubZero™ V-Series is a vertically mounted, multi-stage cryogenic centrifugal pump designed to be submerged directly into LNG storage tanks. By integrating the pump directly into the tank, it eliminates the need for external suction piping, complex priming, and NPSH issues. It is the core component trusted in high-cycle LNG vehicle refueling stations and industrial gas transfer systems.

In-House Manufacturing

Integrated Cryogenic Components

Heavy-Duty Stainless Steel Housings and Manifolds

The SubZero™ system is supported by custom-engineered stainless steel components. Our in-house machining capabilities allow us to manufacture complex cryogenic manifolds, pressure housings, and sensor ports designed to withstand extreme thermal shock and high-pressure LNG flow. Every component is 100% pressure-tested and fully traceable before integration into your system.

  • 304/316L Stainless Steel construction.
  • Custom flange and port configurations (ASME B16.5).
  • Rated for −320°F (−196°C) cryogenic service.

Built to Your Duty Point

Custom-Engineered Configurations

Don't see the exact duty point your project requires? Cryovanta specializes in custom-engineering cryogenic pumping systems to match your exact site and vessel specifications. Over 40% of our SubZero™ V-Series deployments are built to custom project specs.

  • Dual-Discharge Manifolds: Engineered with twin outlet ports for specialized LNG fueling skids and high-throughput dual-dispenser stations.
  • High-Head / High-Pressure Trim: Custom multi-stage impeller stacks and Up-Thrust balancing for deep-well storage tanks and high-pressure vaporization systems requiring heads up to 1,020+ ft (310+ m).
  • Custom ASME / ANSI Flanges: Precision-machined top discharge and bottom mounting flanges built to ASME B16.5 Class 150/300 standards to bolt directly into your existing tank manway or suction vessel.
  • Metallurgy & Exotic Alloy Upgrades: Standard 316L Stainless Steel construction with available upgrades to Hastelloy® C-276, Monel®, or Inconel® for specialty industrial gases and corrosive cryogenic media.

Engineering Features

01

Multi-Stage Design

Optimized hydraulic profile with multi-stage impellers to achieve high differential pressure within a compact vertical footprint.

02

Submerged Operation

Sits directly inside the LNG tank. Eliminates cavitation, NPSH issues, and external piping leaks.

03

Tie-Rod Construction

Precision-machined stainless steel stages held together by high-tensile tie rods for structural integrity at cryogenic temperatures.

04

100% Fully Certified

ATEX, IECEx, CSA, and ISO 9001 certified for global hazardous-area deployment.

05

Zero-Prime-Loss

Designed for high-cycle start/stop operations commonly found in LNG fueling dispensers.
